## Telegram

**Otya 1.0 is getting closer.**

We’re now in the final reliability-validation stage before the first public release.

The work at this stage is not about adding more features. It is about making the experience already inside Otya more dependable — local music and video, playback, Next, account access, updates, Online Music, Private, Transfer, notifications and the services around them.

We are also simplifying Next so users can talk to one assistant while Otya handles approved AI models/providers behind the scenes.

A successful automated build is not enough for us to call Otya ready. The remaining real-device and live-service checks still have to pass first.

More as validation progresses.

## LinkedIn

**Otya 1.0 has entered final reliability validation.**

At PeterSmart Link, we are deliberately holding feature expansion while we validate and harden the first public Otya release.

The current work is focused on the reliability of existing product flows: Android release integrity, architecture-correct updates, authentication sessions, local media playback, Online Music requests, notifications, Private file handling, Transfer, and the latency and resilience of Next.

We are also simplifying the AI architecture. Users should interact with one assistant — Next — rather than having to understand model or provider choices. Otya can route requests through approved providers behind that stable product experience while keeping local playback independent from online AI availability.

We are treating CI, signed builds, real-device acceptance and public release as separate evidence stages. Otya 1.0 will only be described as released once the approved artifact and public Help/Trust information have passed the remaining acceptance checks.

**Otya — by PeterSmart Link**
