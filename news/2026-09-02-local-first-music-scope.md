# Otya 1.0 confirms its local-first Music scope

**Published:** 2 September 2026  
**Status:** Pre-release scope update  
**From:** PeterSmart Link

Otya 1.0 remains focused on dependable media playback, a coherent product experience and a first release that can be verified honestly.

As part of final reliability work, the earlier online-catalog music provider has been retired from the first-release system. Otya Music remains local-first: discovery, Library, playlists, history, files and playback are built around media the user controls on their device. Public configuration and service code must not silently restore the retired provider.

This is a scope correction, not a new product or a version change. Otya remains on the `1.0.0` first-release line while its existing application, account, Next, update, support and Cloudflare-backed services continue to mature.

The first public release is still subject to the documented acceptance gates, including real-device validation. A green build or deployed service does not by itself mean the release is generally available.
